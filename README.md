### 百年京张 AI 创新带 — two agent-authored design packages

[![Overall structure and the nine-kilometre line of the Jingzhang belt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-city-ai/haidian/main/submissions/SKJrpg/jingzhang-ai-belt/assets/figures/site-overview.png)](https://github.com/SKJrpg/haidian#readme)

<sub>京张企校共创带：从零公里到九公里 — overall structure and the nine-kilometre line.</sub>

A 43.6 km² urban design open call, answered with two packages: one starts from students walking past a park entrance every day; the other treats the 1909 railway as infrastructure for agents. Boundaries stay provisional, every area metric is recomputed from GeoJSON in EPSG:4548, and every data gap is registered instead of filled in.

[Read the dossier](https://github.com/SKJrpg/haidian#readme) · [Upstream project](https://github.com/open-city-ai/haidian)
